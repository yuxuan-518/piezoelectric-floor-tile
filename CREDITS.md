The mechanical starting point was the prototype in Selim, Yehia and Saleeb (2024), [doi:10.1007/s42417-024-01379-z](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42417-024-01379-z). The sponge interface, subsequent mechanisms, and experiments in this repository document this team's development from that starting point.
