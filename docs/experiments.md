The oscilloscope presents a finite input impedance, hence the retained term **quasi-open-circuit**. Voltage measurements under this condition are not delivered load energy.

## Stored-energy calculation

For each trial:

\[
\Delta E_C = \frac{1}{2}C\left(V_1^2 - V_0^2\right).
\]

With C = 4.7 μF and voltage in volts, ΔE in μJ equals `2.35 * (V1**2 - V0**2)`. This is the net increase in capacitor energy over the measurement window. It is not the total mechanical energy supplied or all electrical energy generated by the disc.

The copied voltage display for the single-step sheet has one decimal place. The workbook's recorded energy can have been calculated from more precise cell values. Re-squaring the rounded voltage can therefore disagree with its recorded energy. The repository keeps both displayed quantities and calculates descriptive summaries from the **recorded energy** column. It verifies that these energy values fit within the voltage-rounding intervals for a 4.7 μF capacitor.

## Difference from the older summary

The “条件” sheet still describes three groups and contains earlier single-step summary values. Its A energy means (roughly 2.3–2.5 μJ) differ from the current labeled single-step blocks (roughly 10–13 μJ per group). Its B values also differ from the expanded current blocks. Consequently, its earlier improvement figures are not used as current results.

The repository's [summary.csv](../data/ab-comparison/summary.csv) is recomputed from the current labeled blocks. It gives approximately **11.6 μJ for A and 72.5 μJ for B**, with a ratio of means around **6.23**. This is a descriptive comparison of the stored-energy records. The chart shows sample SD rather than confidence intervals. Subject-level independence, randomized order, and a measured input-energy match have not been established here.

The condition sheet mentions 50-step charging and a 150-cycle durability test. No separate populated records for those tests were identified in the six available sheets, so those tests are not presented as completed results.

## Historical records

The historical release archive contains the separately documented eight-disc V1 and V2-era oscilloscope images, capacitor recordings, and motion videos. Older reports discuss a 2.2 μF circuit; that historical value is not substituted for the confirmed 4.7 μF A/B capacitor. Original V1 images show an X10 scope setting and V2 images X1; the archived notes preserve this distinction.

The team confirmed that A/B attenuation settings match. This does not identify the exact circuitry or sensor count in every older historical image. Historical shots are therefore not silently merged into the A/B dataset.
